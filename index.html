About

I am a senior at Grambling State University double majoring in Computer Science and Cybersecurity. My research experience spans across two research projects, the most recent being a data science research at the Georgia Institute of Technology's Center for Health and Humanitarian Systems under Dr. Arthi Rao and Dr. Akane Fujimoto. My interests lie in climate modeling and environmental sustainability using artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Research Interests

  • Machine learning for climate extremes: forecasting droughts and other extreme weather events
  • Climate risk in data-sparse regions, with a focus on Sub-Saharan Africa
  • Early-warning and decision-support systems for disaster management and food security
  • Statistical and generative downscaling of climate data
  • Remote sensing for climate and water-resource monitoring
  • Spatial statistics for the social and health impacts of climate change
